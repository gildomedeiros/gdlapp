Practical proposals only
1. Prioritize explaining and diagnosing lost GPS updates. Distinguish radio/sender gaps from Wi-Fi bridging and inspect antenna/orientation/placement against synchronized surf observations. The present captures show meaningful gaps but do not establish their hardware cause or bound future outages.
2. If backward travel during an outage is the concern, consider maxStartsWithoutFreshGps=0. It prevents new stale-GPS periods; an already running fresh-GPS period still finishes its timer. This does not stop fresh-GPS repeats or solve old-coordinate aiming. No setting was changed.
3. If Return to central should provide a resting position, consider an explicit post-return hold/rearm condition. These logs show immediate departure after both returns. For constant chasing, consider whether a larger approach margin or simpler movement policy better matches the intended shot; test one change at a time against video.
4. Review the two-second surfer reversal block using synchronized video, especially close passes and GPS reacquisition. There is concrete zero-yaw evidence at a 49.59-degree computed error; wholesale increases in yaw speed are not the first conclusion.
5. Investigate RELEASE_UNCONFIRMED after RTH separately from flight movement tuning. Capture authority/disable callbacks through a complete RTH/landing cycle to establish why release is unconfirmed.

No code, settings or release changes are proposed as already implemented. No flight was performed by this analysis.
