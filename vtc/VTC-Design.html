Android screens and workers

Track shows the connection, calibration and aiming states. Calibrate sets the landmark reference. Setup manages endpoints and options. Wave Ride sets the offshore heading and ride detection. Logs, Live Map and Camera Preview show diagnostic output.

WorkerResponsibilityWhere it can wait
Android UI and foreground serviceOwn setup edits, control buttons and status display. The service owns the hardware session and uses a wake lock and Wi-Fi lock while a device session is active.Long operations run on workers. The activity can reconnect to the same service after returning to the app.
vtc-gpsReceive UDP GPS, validate packets and publish the newest accepted fix plus recent observations.WAIT for a GPS datagram on its own socket. Accepted fixes can arrive while the mount worker is waiting on GTi.
vtc-mountOwn routine GTi motion. The scheduled tick runs with a 100 ms delay after completion. A GPS arrival can also queue a tracking tick. A tick may reuse an accepted fix for a new decision.WAIT for synchronous GTi exchanges. Commands in a single tick can span several GPS arrivals.

IDStepMount worker · decisionsGTi and timing
AIM-01Tap Start Aiming.Require GTi, valid calibration and site data, saved offshore heading and fresh accepted GPS. Read both axes stopped. Set the aiming session active. A rider outside the allowed range starts in WAITING TARGET.WAIT for a full position read before arming. Recording and automatic zoom see the active aiming session on their separate worker.
AIM-02Inspect GPS and any active command.Recovery runs first when pending. Otherwise check GPS availability and rider range. With a usable cached pan heading, check whether to stop an active command before polling. PR2 and PR4 hold the opposing direction only.An indicated Stop sends :K1 and returns to the control flow without waiting for the Stop reply.
AIM-03Read pan and do the pan decision.Read pan when a newer acceptable GPS fix and panPollMs permit it. Use the newest GPS available after the read. Compute the rider bearing, framing-adjusted target and current camera aim. AIM-04Choose GoTo, continuous correction or hold.For a nonzero permitted correction, aim error at or above acquisitionTriggerMetres selects finite GoTo. AIM-05Read tilt and do the tilt decision.After pan has had its decision and command opportunity, read tilt when due and use the newest GPS. Pan rule reference

The mount is at the centre of the half-clock. 12 o’clock is the camera’s current aim. VTC compares the previous and newest usable GPS bearings around the mount against that aim. The framing lead uses the separate ride baseline. The pan rule uses the rider’s position, so the signed lead can affect the eventual correction without changing the reported PR number.

RuleRider movingPath on the half-clockVTC behavior
PR1Anticlockwise12 → 11 → 10, away from centreUse normal aiming for an anticlockwise follow. The calculated target, tolerance and limits determine the move.
PR2Anticlockwise2 → 1 → 12, toward centreHold clockwise correction only. Stop an opposing clockwise command. Let an existing anticlockwise pan continue.
PR3Clockwise12 → 1 → 2, away from centreUse normal aiming for a clockwise follow. The calculated target, tolerance and limits determine the move.
PR4Clockwise10 → 11 → 12, toward centreHold anticlockwise correction only. Stop an opposing anticlockwise command. Let an existing clockwise pan continue.

A stationary bearing or unavailable prior fix can leave the pan rule blank. VTC logs continue_existing_pan, stop_opposing_pan or suppress_opposing_start when the directional rule branches. The rule can apply during or outside a ride. Its inputs are GPS and camera heading.

GTi commands and reply waits

GTi uses UDP. N is 1 for pan or 2 for tilt. Each request ends in carriage return, shown as \r in logs. The response is matched by reply shape on the request socket. The six-digit position reply itself carries no axis ID.

Finite tracking GoTo

IDStepAcknowledgedNo-wait
GOTO-01Program motion mode and direction.Send :GN…. WAIT for an empty acknowledgment. Leg magnitude above 1° uses high-speed GoTo mode 0. Otherwise use mode 2.Send :GN…. The reply is collected in the background.
GOTO-02Program the target counter.Send :SN…. WAIT for an empty acknowledgment. A rejected or unusable setter prevents Start.Send :SN…. Continue without waiting for the target reply.
GOTO-03Recheck GPS immediately before Start.Re-evaluate fresh GPS, range, target, direction and relevant gates. If the latest fix changes the compatible destination, reprogram the target and check again. A hold, direction or tolerance change can suppress Start.Same final GPS check and possible setter rewrite. Network replies may still be arriving from earlier sends.
GOTO-04Start pan or tilt.Send :JN. Log tracking_start_sent at transmission. WAIT for Start reply.Send :JN and log tracking_start_sent. Reply collection continues separately.
GOTO-05Observe what the mount does.Track later encoder movement. Two unchanged intervals can mark the move settled and log the difference from the programmed target. No routine motor status query precedes each live Start.Same encoder observation. No-wait pan can attempt a replacement while VTC still believes a finite pan is active. Acknowledged pan and tilt avoid replacing an active finite command unless its command state has cleared or a Stop is in progress.

A Start is a send event. An acknowledgment on a Start socket means a matching reply shape was received. Position change shows motion, and the final target error shows whether it got where VTC intended.

Continuous slew

IDStepVTC calculationGTi exchange
SLEW-01Choose a rate.With a positive acquisition trigger and an eligible smaller error, the planner uses proportional low-speed correction or one fixed high-speed rate. It applies tolerance, restart and high-speed hysteresis. It reduces the rate near the configured limit. An unchanged request issues no update.On first use, WAIT for :bN timer and, when needed, :gN ratio. Those are cached by the GTi client.
SLEW-02Check the axis before changing the command.VTC verifies status. A direction change, low/high mode change or changed high-speed period while moving requires a Stop and stopped status before programming the new rate.WAIT for :fN. If changing mode or high-speed period, :LN then :fN until stopped, up to four checks with 50 ms intervals.
SLEW-03Set mode and step period, then Start if needed.Mode 1 is low-speed slew. Mode 3 is high-speed slew. A same-direction low-speed update can change the period while moving.WAIT for each needed :GN, :IN, :JN reply. Continuous slew always uses acknowledged exchanges.

The acquisition trigger was 0 m in the 1.0.48 field runs. Those runs exercised the finite path. maxPanningSpeed caps the continuous request, while gotoMaxMove caps the finite leg size.

GTi command reference

IDWire commandExpected replyDefault deadline and use
GTI-01:j1\r, :j2\rSix hex digits after =100 ms for live tracking and the final reconnect encoder reads, via encoderTimeoutMs. 1,500 ms for ordinary full position reads.
GTI-02:f1\r, :f2\rThree hex status digits after =1,500 ms. Running, blocked and initialised bits for setup, full position, recovery and slew transitions.
GTI-03:aN\rSix hex digits1,500 ms. Counts per revolution during connect or reconnect.
GTI-04:eN\rSix hex digits1,500 ms for connection exchanges. Read firmware. Discovery runs separately.
GTI-05:FN\r=\r700 ms. Initialise an uninitialised axis on initial Connect.
GTI-06:GN<mode><dir>\r=\r700 ms. Set mode and direction. Finite GoTo uses 0 or 2, continuous slew uses 1 or 3.
GTI-07:SN<target24>\r=\r700 ms. Set an absolute finite target as little-endian hex.
GTI-08:JN\r=\r700 ms. Start the programmed movement. No-wait uses the deadline only for background reply logging.
GTI-09:KN\r=\r700 ms asynchronous reply window. Tracking Stop for one axis, in either GoTo mode.
GTI-10:LN\r=\r700 ms. Stop for the verified Stop path and certain slew transitions. VTC follows this with motor status verification.
GTI-11:bN\rSix hex digits1,500 ms. Read timer frequency for continuous slew.
GTI-12:gN\rTwo hex digits1,500 ms. Read high-speed ratio.
GTI-13:IN<period24>\r=\r700 ms. Set step period for continuous slew.

Full position read. :f1 → :f2 → :j1 → :j2 → :f1 → :f2. Used at Connect, calibration, Start Aiming and some recovery or fixed move paths. A running bit before or after the encoder readings makes the snapshot report moving.

A mismatched reply shape is discarded within the original deadline. An explicit error can end the wait. !2 is reported as motor not stopped. In No-wait, another request may already have gone out when that error gets logged.

Holds, Stop and GTi recovery

IDConditionVTC actionWait or result
STOP-01Tracking needs to stop one axis.PR2 or PR4 can stop an opposing pan. An opposite correction, range or sector block, or GPS gap can request Stop. Send :KN for the affected active axis. If movement is still observed, a retry can be sent after at least 500 ms.No reply wait for the tracking Stop. The default 2 s software reversal timer delays only a new opposite Start, measured from the previous Start or continuous rate command. VTC rechecks on subsequent ticks.
STOP-02Verified full Stop. Used for Connect, Stop Aiming, Disconnect, Exit and some recovery paths.Invalidate pending Starts for an explicit stop. For an active mount, send :L1 → :L2, then check both axes with :f1 → :f2. The full Stop tries up to three times, with up to four status pairs per attempt and 50 ms intervals while moving.WAIT for Stop replies and stopped status. The explicit Stop work is on vtc-stop. Connect runs its own initial full Stop in the connection sequence.
STOP-03GPS becomes stale or rider goes outside range.Stale GPS stops new targeting. On GPS gap expiry, routine tracking may send axis Stop. The watchdog has a separate verified Stop path for continuous motion. An out-of-range fix shows WAITING TARGET and requests Stop for active axes.Aiming normally stays armed and resumes when a fresh, in-range fix returns. The continuous watchdog’s unconfirmed full Stop can disarm. An active finite GoTo can reach the reconnect branch before the routine GPS-gap or range checks.
REC-01A tracking encoder read fails.Use a cache within maxPositionAgeMs. For an expired cache, try a fresh read, then schedule GTi reconnect if it fails. If a pan timeout skips tilt and tilt is already stale, VTC can schedule reconnect directly.WAIT on any extra encoder read, using the configured deadline. tracking_tilt_read_skipped records both cache ages.
REC-02RECONNECTING GTi.Keep aiming armed. On the replacement connection read resolution, status and firmware for pan then tilt. If resolution differs from the established value, reread the same axis once. If a status says uninitialised, check that axis again before declaring reference loss.WAIT for each :aN, :fN, :eN reply. Those exchanges allow 1,500 ms each. An unresolved mismatch or read error schedules another attempt after 500 ms. Two uninitialised statuses trigger recalibration.
REC-03Resume after reconnect.Read both encoders, adopt the replacement client, then check the sectors before continuing tracking. If a sector check was already pending, take the verified Stop and limit path.:j1 → :j2 with the live encoder timeout. tracking_gti_reconnected identifies a successful recovery.
REC-04Encoder position reports outside a configured limit.Request a full Stop while keeping aiming armed. Recheck both axes with a full position read. A fresh, stopped, in-limit position permits resuming. A confirmed violation stays stopped and is checked again after about 1 s. A failed recheck goes through reconnect.WAIT for the independent full Stop and then for each command in the position snapshot.
REC-05Full Stop cannot be verified.Report STOP UNCONFIRMED and block further movement on the explicit-stop path. For Stop & Exit, the service remains available for retry. Limit recovery instead schedules reconnect.A Stop acknowledgment and stopped status are separate observations.

Canon camera sequence

Camera work runs on one serial worker. It takes the GTi Wi-Fi network when available, or the LoRa network when that is the active link. The regular poll is scheduled 2 s after the previous poll finishes. HTTP connection and read deadlines are each 1,500 ms for normal camera requests.

IDActionVTC behaviorCanon exchange
CAM-01Find Camera or enter its IP.Probe the saved endpoint first, then test peers on the active subnet if needed. Show verified Canon model and IP. Selecting a result stores that endpoint.Background discovery GET /ccapi/ver100/deviceinformation. Default camera port is 8080.
CAM-02Poll the camera.Read Canon events and run recording control, then read zoom. Successful zoom readback sets the CAM connection state and records the confirmed optical zoom. VTC also checks and synchronises the camera clock to the phone.WAIT on camera worker for GET /event/polling, GET /shooting/control/zoom, and separate clock requests to /functions/datetime.
CAM-03Automatic zoom during aiming.Bands selects the configured range-band base zoom and applies the current scale. Auto-fit requests zoom from range × wideFrameFactor ÷ (desiredFrameWidthMetres ÷ scale). The request is capped by the optical and CCAPI limits. Readback keeps running after Stop Aiming and the lens is left at its last setting.WAIT for POST /shooting/control/zoom when a change is needed. Matching zoom readback confirms lens position. Frame width is estimated from range, the wide-frame factor and confirmed optical zoom.
CAM-04Start recording when aiming is armed.If VTC has no recording start time, request Start. Use a successful Start reply or a Canon Start event to establish the timer. A specific unsupported-mode response can trigger Movie Mode and another Start.WAIT for POST /shooting/control/recbutton with action start. Camera requests are separate from GTi moves.

CAM-07Camera Preview → Update.Queue one snapshot on the camera worker. Enable the small preview, fetch a JPEG and turn preview off. Display the last captured image and update time.WAIT on the camera worker for the preview HTTP exchanges.

Reading one decision in order

  1. Find gps_accepted by sequence, then aim_cycle or tracking_axis_decision for its range, target, pan rule and requested direction.
  2. Check tracking_start_sent or tracking_goto_not_started. Link to mount_tx, mount_rx, mount_rx_discarded and mount_reply_missing using command, transaction and local UDP port. A reconnect may reuse transaction numbers.
  3. Compare successive :j1 reports or tracking_first_encoder_movement. tracking_goto_observed_settled gives the remaining counter error. The encoder sampling interval and possible wrong-axis replies affect what the log can establish.
  4. If pan paused, read the recorded reason and check for explicit Stop, reversal, range, a cached encoder read or reconnect. Use the GPS and camera times to locate the clip.
